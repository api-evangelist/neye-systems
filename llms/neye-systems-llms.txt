# nEye (nEye Systems)

> nEye (nEye.ai, formerly nEye Systems) is a silicon photonics company in Santa Clara and
> Emeryville, California building an optical circuit switch on a chip — a programmable photonic
> integrated circuit combining silicon photonics, MEMS and CMOS to switch light directly between
> GPUs, CPUs and memory inside AI and high-performance computing data centers. Spun out of UC
> Berkeley research by Ming C. Wu; led by CEO Ashish Vengsarkar. $152M raised, including an $80M
> Series C led by Sutter Hill Ventures (April 2026). Participates in the Open Compute Project
> Open OCS effort with Google, NVIDIA and Microsoft.
